# Salaries & payslips

Payroll is separate from the broadly shared Finance workspace. Only active users with the **admin** or **finance** role can prepare payroll or read salary settings, payroll totals, payment references and activity. Only administrators can approve payroll. Other active personal accounts can read only their own released payslips. Shared accounts have no payroll access. Module-access overrides do not grant payroll management rights.

1. Open **Salaries & payslips → Salary settings** and configure each employee in MWK. Existing staff and HR details are reused. Enter monthly basic pay, overtime, bonus and manually named cash allowances (name and amount for each). PAYE and pension calculate automatically. Salary-advance recovery and other deductions are entered manually; employer pension is an additional company contribution and does not reduce take-home pay.
2. Create the month's payroll. Enabled settings for active staff become independent snapshots. Later salary changes do not alter existing runs. One run per calendar month prevents duplicate monthly payroll.
3. Review each employee's amounts. Add a note explaining adjustments. Submit for approval; an administrator can approve or return it for correction. Submission confirms that deductions have been reviewed. PAYE uses the 2026 Malawi monthly bands below; pension uses the standard minimum 5% employee / 10% employer scheme described below.
4. Record actual payments against an approved employee line, including date, method and reference. Partial payments are supported. This records payments; it does not move money. Overpayments, future payment dates and duplicate request IDs are rejected or handled idempotently. Recorded payments cannot be edited or deleted through the application; investigate a mistake before adding another payment.
5. Release fully paid payslips individually or in a batch. Employees can view and print/save their own CAGE-branded payslips as PDF using the browser print dialog. Automatic email requires the separately enabled delivery setup below. Approved amounts and released payslips are immutable. Managers can preview and print an unreleased line as a clearly marked internal draft, with the recorded payroll creation time. The issued payslip uses the round CAGE stamp from quotations and invoices, dated from its recorded release time in Malawi time (CAT). A stable slot derived from the payslip ID varies placement in the blank lower-right footer area across issued slips without covering document information. The date and placement do not change on reprint at the same page size. An internal draft is not proof of payment.

Gross = basic + total of named cash allowances + overtime + bonus. Allowance totals and PAYE are calculated on the server; a client-supplied PAYE or aggregate allowance total cannot override named entries. Cash allowances, overtime and bonuses contribute to the PAYE base. Pension, advance recovery and other employee deductions are taken after PAYE and do not reduce that base. This cash-payroll calculation does not classify benefits in kind or reimbursements.

Monthly PAYE uses marginal bands: the first K170,000 at 0%; the next K1,400,000 at 30%; the next K8,430,000 at 35%; and earnings above K10,000,000 at 40%. For example, taxable earnings of K1,000,000 produce PAYE of K249,000. Rates are checked against the [MRA public notice](https://www.mra.mw/admin/storage/download_files/1767952247_NEW%20TAX%20MEASURES...pdf). Amounts are rounded to two decimal places. The applied rule identifier and taxable earnings are saved with each salary/payroll snapshot. New payroll months before January 2026 are rejected because this version has no earlier tax schedule. Future changes to rules must be implemented explicitly; existing approved payslips retain their saved figures.

Each employee may have up to 50 cash allowance entries, with a custom name (up to 100 characters) and a nonnegative amount. Named entries appear separately on payslips, with a total-allowances subtotal. Older aggregate allowance amounts display as “Other allowance” when edited; approved historical payslips remain unchanged.

 Deductions = PAYE + employee pension + salary advance recovery + other deductions. Net = gross − deductions. Zero take-home pay is permitted; negative net pay is rejected. Amounts are rounded to two decimal places on the server. No attendance-based deductions or automatic loan balances are created.

Salary settings can be disabled for future runs. Employee amounts can be adjusted in a draft run. Existing payment history remains private to payroll managers, and the employee's payslip shows only their own amounts, cumulative paid amount and payment completion date. Activity records capture actor, action and before/after details. Salary settings, payroll lines and bank/payment data are never written to the shared workspace or local draft recovery storage.

Implementation: dedicated RLS-protected tables, read-only client grants, authenticated public SECURITY INVOKER RPC wrappers over checked functions in an unexposed `payroll_private` schema. Mutations lock the run and validate its revision; monetary totals and transition permissions are enforced in PostgreSQL. No service key is included in the browser.

Validation: `npm run check`, `npm run build`, and the rollback-only SQL script `validation/payroll.sql` and `validation/payroll-tax.sql` against the deployed schema, plus `node validation/payroll.cjs` for screen checks. The SQL script creates temporary test staff inside a transaction and checks role isolation, workflow, arithmetic, stale writes, partial payments and publication. All test changes are rolled back.

## Automatic pension

The standard minimum defined-contribution scheme uses 5% employee and 10% employer contributions on pensionable emoluments under section 12 of the [Pension Act 2023](https://www.labour.gov.mw/images/documents/Malawi-Pension-Act_2023.pdf). Employee pension is an after-PAYE deduction; the employer contribution is an additional company cost. These calculations record liabilities, not actual remittances to a pension fund.

Basic salary is pensionable. Each cash allowance has an explicit pension treatment: regular contractual allowance, qualifying housing excess, or excluded. The 2025 Pensionable Emoluments Directive includes regular contractual cash allowances; qualifying housing counts only to the extent the combined housing allowance exceeds 40% of guaranteed basic wages, provided it is not in lieu of accommodation. Housing paid in lieu of accommodation must be marked Excluded. Business travel, fuel/car-in-lieu, utilities, subsistence and the directive's other excluded allowances must be classified accordingly. Overtime and performance/discretionary bonuses are excluded by this implementation. Contractual schemes requiring broader pensionable earnings, higher contribution rates, employer-paid employee contributions, exemptions or defined-benefit treatment need separate configuration; do not represent them with the standard-minimum calculation.

All new saves, run creation and draft edits calculate pension on the server; submitted numeric employee/employer pension amounts cannot override the result. Pensionable earnings, rates and rule ID are snapshotted with each saved employee line. Existing approved figures are preserved. No pension cash has been transferred.

For a basic-only employee with no other deductions, basic salary K2,450,833.34 produces PAYE K728,291.67 and employee pension K122,541.67, leaving exactly K1,600,000.00 after rounding. Employer pension is K245,083.33, making salary plus employer pension K2,695,916.67. See validation/payroll-pension.sql for the regression.

## Automatic payslip email

For an enabled organization, newly released payslips with a positive, fully recorded payment are queued. Payroll approval and release are still explicit Finance controls. There is no import or retroactive queue. Managers see **Queued**, **Sending**, **Sent**, **Failed** or **Needs review** on the monthly payroll. **Sent** means the accounting mail server accepted the message, not that the employee read it.

Each attachment comes from its released payroll snapshot, not a file uploaded by an administrator. The Hub requires the employee's active, nonshared profile email to match the verified sign-in address and rechecks it at dispatch; unverified, duplicate or changed addresses fail closed. The worker uses the official logo, payroll amounts, existing round CAGE stamp artwork, the original CAT issue timestamp and a stable lower-right slot. The queue never stores a salary breakdown or PDF. Only payroll managers can see delivery status. Requests and logs must not contain salary figures, SMTP credentials, or attachment contents.

### Staged activation

1. Apply both payroll email migrations and deploy the `payroll-email-dispatch` Edge Function. Leave delivery disabled. Confirm the same verified-TLS SMTP host and port 465 shown in the accounting mailbox's connection settings; the SMTP login, envelope and From address are fixed to `accounting@cagemw.com`. This does not change webmail or other Hub email settings.
2. In the **Supabase Edge Function Secrets** dashboard, set `PAYROLL_SMTP_HOST`, `PAYROLL_SMTP_PASSWORD` (accounting mailbox only), `PAYROLL_EMAIL_ORG_ID`, `PAYROLL_EMAIL_CRON_SECRET` (at least 32 random characters) and `PAYROLL_EMAIL_TEST_ENABLED=true`. Do not put the password into chat, SQL, repository, or frontend. Keep `PAYROLL_EMAIL_LIVE` absent or false. Add the matching cron secret and project URL in Vault as `payroll_email_cron_secret` and `payroll_email_project_url`. No staff mails can go out from this setup alone.
3. Have the deployment operator call the function with the secret header and `{"mode":"test"}`. Its only recipient is Alexander's CAGE address and it uses a fictional PDF. Verify receipt, From header, attachment and presentation before setting `PAYROLL_EMAIL_TEST_ENABLED=false`. A successful SMTP response alone is not proof of receipt.
4. After the test is verified, configure exactly the CAGE organization in `payroll_email_settings` with a first-period limit of **2026-12-01 or later**, then enable that row and set `PAYROLL_EMAIL_LIVE=true`. Check that November remains excluded and that there are no historical queue rows. The worker sends only rows created by release, one per scheduled invocation, with a unique job per payslip.
5. To pause, disable the settings row and set `PAYROLL_EMAIL_LIVE=false`. An already in-flight message cannot be recalled. Do not reset **Needs review** automatically: compare the stable Message-ID (`<payroll-ITEM_UUID@cagemw.com>`) with mail logs first. For confirmed non-delivery, Finance may send the released PDF privately after correcting the recipient or have an operator requeue the same job after rechecking the same verified address. Do not bulk-resend.

Credential setup is deliberately separate from code deployment. A browser session alone does not grant the Hub SMTP credentials.
